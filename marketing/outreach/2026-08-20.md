# Prospects — 20 August 2026

> **Rewritten on 20 August, evening.** The first version of every message
> below opened on the prospect's web address ("jullie site staat op
> x.jouwweb.nl"). True, and it sold nothing: a salon owner reads that and
> thinks "so what". Each message now opens on the phone ringing while
> their hands are in a customer's hair, names the customer they lost
> without ever knowing it, and only then shows what they get. The
> subdomain has moved to one closing line. Because the pitch is now the
> booking system, the price in nine of the ten is 549, not 299. See
> "How to write the message" in `agents/outreach-agent.md`.

Sectors: kapsalons, schoonheidssalons, hoveniers. Cities: spread across NL.
Count: 10.

**The signal, and why it is solid.** Every business below runs its website
on a free builder subdomain: `iets.wixsite.com` or `iets.jouwweb.nl`. The
weakness is the web address itself, so it needed no page visit to
establish. It is also a real, expensive problem for them, not a matter of
taste:

- They cannot have `info@hunbedrijf.nl`. Their business mail is a gmail
  address, which reads as amateur to anyone comparing two salons.
- In Google results their address shows as `jen741.wixsite.com` rather
  than their own name.
- A subdomain inherits none of its own authority, so they rank below
  competitors with an own domain for the same search.
- If the builder raises its price or closes the account, the site and
  everything in it is gone. They own nothing.

**Owner check before sending, thirty seconds each.** Open the URL on your
phone. Confirm the address bar still shows the builder subdomain and that
the business is still trading. If they have since moved to their own
domain, skip them and mark the row `not fit`.

Nothing below claims anything about their design, speed or mobile
behaviour. Those pages were never opened, and guessing would be
fabrication.

**Which package to quote.** A site alone is the starterswebsite at 299
euro. The moment a booking system is part of it, where a customer picks
their own slot, it is the bedrijfswebsite at 549. Nine of the ten below
can take bookings, and every one of those nine messages sells the booking
system, so all nine quote 549 outright. Only the gardener, who has
nothing to book, quotes 299. Selling a booking system and naming 299
would be a promise the invoice cannot keep.

## Onderwerpregels

Elk onderwerp noemt iets dat alleen voor dat ene bedrijf geldt. Wie zijn
eigen webadres in de onderwerpregel ziet staan, weet meteen dat dit geen
rondzendbericht is.

- Onder de vijftig tekens, anders kapt een telefoon hem af.
- Geen hoofdletters, geen uitroeptekens, geen woord als gratis of
  aanbieding. Dat zijn de patronen waar spamfilters op letten.
- Nooit tien keer hetzelfde onderwerp op een dag. Verschillende
  onderwerpen houden het verzendadres schoon.
- Veilige terugval als een onderwerp niet werkt: "Over de website van
  [naam]". Saai, maar het komt aan.

## Send order

Start with 6 (Sittard, nearby), then 3 and 1 (a name change and a
personal-handle subdomain are the sharpest openings). The rest in any
order.

Send six today, not ten. Ten identical-feeling messages in one hour read
as a blast even when each one is different.
