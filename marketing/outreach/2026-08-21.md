**Format.** Per prospect the subject line and the message body are in two
separate copy blocks, so both can be pasted without editing. Where a
business email could be found in public sources it is listed under
**E-mail**; where none is published that is stated plainly rather than
guessed at.

**Rotation.** The two previous runs were salons, nail studios, dog groomers
and massage. None of those appear here. Seven of the eight are
booking-shaped and quote 549; the klusbedrijf has nothing to book, so that
message sells finished work and quotes 299.

**On the Limburg directive.** Sam's standing orders say to start close to
home. I searched Maastricht, Meerssen, Valkenburg, Heerlen, Kerkrade,
Brunssum, Roermond, Venlo, Weert and Sittard for garages and rijscholen on
free builder subdomains and found none: the Limburg businesses in these two
sectors that search surfaces are on their own domains (Vakgarage Lennaerts,
Mengelers, Rijschool Akkermans, Autorijschool Jos Janssen, Augustin,
AnyDrive). They are not prospects — having an own domain is the thing we
sell. Batch 2 on 20 August did place one Limburg prospect (Munstergeleen).
Rather than force a weak Limburg entry, this run went to the businesses
where the signal is real. Sectors rotate; the province follows the signal.

**The signal.** Every business below runs its site on a free builder
subdomain (`iets.jouwweb.nl` or `iets.wixsite.com`), which search
establishes without opening the page. On those plans there is no booking
system, so for the seven booking-shaped businesses the subdomain is also a
reliable proxy for "a customer cannot book without phoning".

Two exceptions, and they matter. For **Autorijschool Jan Boezen** and
**Klusbedrijf de Jong** business directories list an own domain
(`autorijschooljanboezen.nl`, `klussenbedrijfdejong.nl`) alongside the
builder subdomain. Directory data is often stale, and in de Jong's case
that domain also appears attached to a different company (Klussenbedrijf
Corstiaan de Jong in Zuidhorn), so it may well be a mismatch. It cannot be
settled from here. Both messages have therefore been written so that the
argument does not rest on the domain at all, and the closing line about a
jouwweb-address has been removed from both. Check the domain before
sending; if it is live and theirs, the message still stands as written.

Nothing below claims anything about design, speed or mobile behaviour. The
network here blocks outbound requests to these domains, so no page was
opened and guessing would be fabrication. Every observation is marked
`(from search)`.

**Owner check, thirty seconds each.** Open the URL on your phone. Confirm
the address bar still shows the builder subdomain, that the business is
trading, and that there is no online booking already. If any of the three
fails, skip them and mark the row `not fit`. While that page is open, the
email address is usually right there — that is the cheapest moment to pick
up the six that search would not give me.

**Deliberately skipped.** `205-autoservice.jouwweb.nl` describes itself as
a hobby of about twelve years keeping the Peugeot 205 alive, not a trading
garage. `skodapopma.jouwweb.nl` sells Skoda parts, which is webshop-shaped
rather than booking-shaped, and no business address was findable.
